Case Study

Valet Ballet

Valet Ballet is the umbrella product family for the Valet apps. It organizes mobile and desktop tools for tracking goods, services, and operational records in workflows where accuracy and traceability matter more than visual flash.

Role Platform framing, systems design, product storytelling
Focus Cross-platform records, goods and services tracking, operational clarity
Outcome A product family that supports both mobile action and desktop oversight

The Challenge

Operational handoffs around goods and services often depend on incomplete notes, inconsistent tracking, and scattered tools. The challenge was to define a product family that keeps those records understandable and usable across different contexts.

The Approach

The work established Valet Ballet as the organizing layer for apps like Pull-Tab Valet, Pull-Tab Valet Desktop, and DVD Valet. The shared direction emphasized simple interfaces, trustworthy records, and a clean split between in-the-moment mobile actions and richer desktop review.

What It Demonstrates

  • Building a coherent product family rather than disconnected one-off tools
  • Designing across desktop and mobile as one connected operational system
  • Reducing ambiguity with clearer records, workflows, and product boundaries
