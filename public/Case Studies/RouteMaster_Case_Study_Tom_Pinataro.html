Case Study

RouteMaster

RouteMaster is the generic field-service parent app behind the Steward products. It was built to make route work, daily tasks, and field status easier to manage from a mobile dashboard before being specialized into industry-specific versions.

Role Product architecture, workflow design, reusable app pattern
Focus Field routes, mobile task flow, offsite coordination
Outcome A reusable foundation for multiple route-based service apps

The Challenge

Field teams need route context, daily assignments, and status visibility in one place. The challenge was to create a mobile-first product pattern that could support offsite work cleanly enough to be reused across different service businesses.

The Approach

The work focused on the essentials of route management: clear visit lists, straightforward task handling, check-in/check-out flow, notes, and lightweight mobile navigation. From there, the structure was adapted into verticalized apps like Bloom Steward and Pool Steward.

What It Demonstrates

  • Creating a reusable product base instead of rebuilding the same workflow from scratch
  • Designing around how field technicians actually move through the day
  • Building a system that can support multiple specialized operational apps
