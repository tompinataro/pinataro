Case Study

Pull-Tab Valet

Pull-Tab Valet is a mobile app for tracking pull-tab sales and sessions with a cleaner operational workflow. It supports venue-based work, UPC scanning, closeouts, and reporting in a format that stays usable on the floor.

Role Product framing, mobile workflow design, reporting UX
Focus Sales sessions, UPC scanning, closeouts, summary reports
Outcome A cleaner mobile flow for a specialized sales-tracking process

The Challenge

Pull-tab operations involve venue context, box tracking, closeouts, and report generation. When those pieces live in memory or scattered tools, small errors become expensive and hard to audit.

The Approach

The product was shaped around the real workflow: choose a venue, scan pull-tab box UPCs, complete closeouts, auto-calculate totals, and generate reports that can be reviewed or emailed. The emphasis was on making each step obvious without overcomplicating the interface.

What It Demonstrates

  • Building software for a niche but very real operational workflow
  • Supporting scanning, closeouts, and reports in one mobile product
  • Reducing cognitive load by making the next action clear at each step
