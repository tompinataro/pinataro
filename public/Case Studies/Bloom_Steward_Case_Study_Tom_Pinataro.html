Case Study

Bloom Steward

Bloom Steward is a custom accountability and quality-control application built for a live plant leasing and maintenance company. It was designed to streamline field technician workflows, improve data tracking, and keep service standards visible in daily operations.

Role Solo build, product design, full-stack implementation
Focus Plant maintenance routes, quality control, field accountability
Outcome A production app for route-based maintenance work in the field

The Challenge

Field maintenance teams need clear route assignments, dependable visit tracking, and a simple way to log quality-control work without getting buried in admin overhead. The product needed to support daily plant service while keeping standards and follow-through visible.

The Approach

Bloom Steward focused on the mobile workflow technicians actually use: open today’s route, review the visit, complete a checklist, check in or out with optional location verification, add notes, and submit the work even when connectivity is unreliable. The system was backed by secure authentication and persisted data with PostgreSQL.

What It Demonstrates

  • Designing and building a custom field-service product end to end
  • Turning quality-control needs into a usable route-based mobile workflow
  • Balancing practical UX, data integrity, and real-world service execution
